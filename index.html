Food cost target: Keep your food cost at 35% or under. Spending $7 of food per plate across 20 plates is a $140 food budget, which equals a 35% food cost.

Example: A $20 plate across 20 customers makes $400 in sales. With a $140 food budget, that leaves a $260 profit at a 35% food cost.

Going over $140? You are free to spend more, but raise your plate price to match. A higher price keeps your food cost at 35% or under and protects your profit and your points. For example, spending $210 means charging at least $30 a plate.

Rules & Points
How a Restaurant Boss grade is earned.

The Challenge

  • 60 minutes of prep, then a 1-hour rush.
  • Serve 20 customers.
  • Up to 5 staff members.
  • A $140 food budget.
  • Contestants set their own plate price.
  • Customers eat free. Sales are pretend, to show how the restaurant would have done.

The Base Score

Four equal parts are averaged: the customer survey score, Judge 1, Judge 2 and Judge 3. These carry all of the weight.

The customer survey score is the average of the five survey questions: customer service rating (1 to 10), food on time (yes), plate rating (1 to 10), would pay the plate price (yes) and would return (yes).

Health Grade Points

The health grade does not count toward the average. It adds or removes points.

A (90 and up)+3
B (80s)+2
C (70s)0
D (60s)-2
F (below 60)-3

Food Cost Points

Food cost = total spent ÷ (20 plates × plate price).

35% or under+2
Over 35%, under 70%0
70% or more (double the limit)-2

Staying at 35%: plate price × 7 is the most you can spend. A $20 plate allows $140. Spending more is allowed, but raise your plate price to match.

Bonus and Penalty Points

Served all 20 customers in the hour+1
Spent $140 or under+1
Did not serve all 20 customers-1

Service Time is the total time used out of the 1-hour clock. It is recorded for bragging rights and does not change the score. A Service Time of 60:00 means the contestant used the whole hour and was cut off.

The Final Grade

Final score = base score + health points + food cost points + bonus and penalty points.

B80 to 89.9
